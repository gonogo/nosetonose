# BOOKING ON A COURSE

To book a course follow the link *Click here to enquire…*
which you’ll find on each course page. Submit the form with
your details and we will let you know about available places and
how to proceed with payment of your deposit.  
Deposits can be paid by cheque, bank tranfer or online. NOTE: Bookings
can only be confirmed after we have received your deposit. As the
booking usually only covers the course fee, please check you know
the total cost of the course (course fee + accommodation and meals).

If you have any questions or problems, please [email
us](mailto:mail@nosetonose.info).

# Confirmation of booking

You are booked on the course **ONLY** when Nose to Nose
confirms your place. We will not take your deposit if the course is
full.

# 

# Waiting list

If the course is full, we will put you on our waiting list. This means
you get priority and we will notify you when a place becomes available
. We will do our best to put you on the next available course.

# 

# Book early

Our commitment to venue costs means we appreciate knowing early how
many will be attending our courses.   

# 

# PAYMENT TERMS Cancellation

If you cancel up to 60 days prior to the course we will refund your
deposit minus a cancellation fee of £20. We regret that no refunds
can be issued after that time unless your place is taken by a replacement.

In the rare event that we should have to cancel a course we would
let you know 14 days before and you get a full refund. If you come
from far, we advise you to take out travel insurance to cover this
possibility.

Please remember to budget for your board and accommodation costs,
which are usually separate from our tuition fees.

As groups are limited to 12 people, places will be allocated in
the order received.
