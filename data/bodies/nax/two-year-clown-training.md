# TWO-YEAR CLOWN TRAINING

# 2013-15

Nose to Nose of North America is pleased to announce its first
two-year program of clown training. Our program provides a coherent
progression from introductory work to developing skills of performance
and Social Clowning.  
  
**SAVE $200 ... or more!**

**ADVANTAGES**

Participation in the full two-year training provides:

- a $50 reduction on each course you attend (usual fee is around
  $490/week)
- guaranteed progression from Clown 1, 2, 3 and Social Clowning
- Personal supervision on your progression.
- for any additional course in our program taken during the time
  of your training, the reduction of $50 applies.

**COURSE OUTLINE**

The training consists of 4 one-week long courses to be completed over
the course of two years.   
Clown 1 - Clown 2 - Clown 3 - Social Clowning  
  

**HOW
TO APPLY**

- Choose the 4 courses that suit you (one week for each of the
  levels) - *[see our course dates](https://www.nosetonose.info/nax/coursedates%20na.htm)*
- Send a deposit of $250.   
  We will refund your deposit in December 2015 or earlier when you
  have completed the full program.   
  *Please note: Your commitment to the program is important. If
  you withdraw from the program before the end of the training in
  2015 you may lose your deposit.*
