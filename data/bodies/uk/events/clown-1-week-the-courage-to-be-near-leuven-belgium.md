*with Vivian Gladwell and An Valenteyn  
Workshop in English*

The word “courage” comes from a root that means “heart.” Through clowning and connecting to our playful spirit we can develop and sustain, in ourselves and each other, the heart to be who we truly are, to trust life and ourselves. “Taking heart” is a profoundly inward process, and an invitation for overcoming the fears that block us from being fully present in the “Here and Now.” This course establishes the ground for all our work and is an essential first step before going on to more advanced courses.  
The group is limited to 12

The workshop ‘Clown I,’ is the discovery of your clown’s home base. It is the first of a series of three workshops. Essential in that phase are mirroring, listening, connecting and expressing the innerworld.

**Facilitators:**

**Vivian Gladwell[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21-300x200.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21.jpg)**

Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.  
Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the [**Bataclown**](http://www.bataclown.com/) in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester ([**Clownanalyst**](http://www.clownanalyse.fr/)).  
He created ***Nose to Nose UK*** in 1990, ***Nose to Nose of North America*** in 2006 and ***Fool View***, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy – [**view history*****.***](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (*South Africa, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…*)

He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.

**An Valenteyn[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/valenteyn-300x138.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/valenteyn.jpg)**

An was born in Belgium where she lives and works. She trained as a social worker and went on to study mime and movement theatre at the [Lassaad International School](https://www.lassaad.com/gb/) in Brussels – a school influenced by the teachings of Jacques Lecoq. 

She became so inspired by clowning as a playful and authentic way of being and living in the moment that in 2006 she joined the [Cliniclown](https://www.cliniclowns.nl/en) team in Belgium working as a hospital clown. She is also a coach and a trainer for new members of the team.  

In 2015 she met [Nose to Nose](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/). She finished the Clown facilitator training in 2023 with Nose to Nose. She also performs as a clown and actor in music theatre.
