**Course description:**Clowns are shapeshifters, able to transform themselves by playfully interpreting the word around them. They can help us to shift our perspectives and awaken empathy by bringing the world alive.

In this unique course, we will guide you through practical exercises and simple improvisational structures to help you cultivate your skills and deepen your understanding of the art of clowning. 

Identification with objects teaches important clowning principles such as:

- commitment to physicality and embodiment,
- use of voice for emotional expression,
- relationship with partners and audience,
- personal freedom
- breaking the rules of theatrical convention
- exploring roles and breaking free from roles

Come play and let your clown discover the world as new and full of opportunity, absurdity, laughter and poignancy. 

This weekend is open to all levels – whether you’re experienced or new to clowning. 

The group is limited to 12 people.

***Facilitators:***

**Abigail Dancey**

[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/de/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/abigail.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/de/events/introduction-to-clowning-dornach-switzerland/abigail)After getting as much clowning experience in the USA over the past ten years as I could– through Nose to Nose, Christopher Bayes at the Pandemonium Studio, as well as through Michael Chekov’s beautiful approach to the clown – I’m excited to be in the facilitator training with Nose to Nose here in Europe!

I currently spend most of my time with two excellent clowns, my three year old and one year old. This is perfect training. Not only am I playing, but I also have the almost constant experience of chaos and failure. However, when I’m not raising children I’ve worked as a therapist in family therapy, for young adults, as well as addiction therapy. I’ve done workshops for young people in creative theater and improvisation who are interested in clowning and the experience of social authenticity. These things I hope to do more of through Nose to Nose as a facilitator.

## *Vivian Gladwell*

*Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.*

*Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at* [***Emerson College***](http://www.emerson.org.uk/clowning) *– UK. He trained as a facilitator with the* [***Bataclown***](http://www.bataclown.com/) *in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester (*[***Clownanalyst***](http://www.bataclown.com/Congres-colloques-journees-professionnelles-AG-seminaires-d-entreprise.html)*).*

*He created* [***Nose to Nose UK***](https://nosetonose.info/uk) *in 1990,* [***Nose to Nose of North America***](https://nosetonose.info/na/) *in 2006,* [***Nose to Nose Germany***](http://www.nosetonose.info/de/) *in 2008,* [***NTN Italy in 2016***](http://www.nosetonose.info/it) *and* ***Fool View****, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy –* [***view history.***](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)*He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (South Africa, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…)*

*He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.*
