*with Vivian Gladwell and Ali Stockford*

The word “courage” comes from a root that means “heart.” Through clowning and connecting to our playful spirit we can develop and sustain, in ourselves and each other, the heart to be who we truly are, to trust life and ourselves. “Taking heart” is a profoundly inward process, and an invitation for overcoming the fears that block us from being fully present in the “Here and Now.” This course establishes the ground for all our work and is an essential first step before going on to more advanced courses.  
The group is limited to 12

***“Nose to Nose is a transformative experience! The gentleness, generosity and care surrounding the playing and clowning makes it such a safe and exhilarating experience. I always leave these workshops feeling like a better person. I am becoming a happier and more confident clown and it is thanks to this special way of working. I can’t wait to be back.” Astrid Olofsson- Actress***

**Facilitators:**

**Vivian Gladwell**

[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21-300x200.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21.jpg)Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.  
Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the [**Bataclown**](http://www.bataclown.com/) in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester ([**Clownanalyst**](http://www.clownanalyse.fr/)).  
He created ***Nose to Nose UK*** in 1990, ***Nose to Nose of North America*** in 2006 and ***Fool View***, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy – **[view history*.*](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)**  
He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (*South Africa, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…*)

He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.

**Alison Stockford**

[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/128838192_10158816587148684_35942922814197665_n-2-257x300.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/128838192_10158816587148684_35942922814197665_n-2.jpg)

**Alison (Ali) Stockford – Clowning**

Ali has been facilitating Nose to Nose Clowning workshops since 2016, teaching across the UK and, more recently, Europe. She holds this work in high esteem as the perfect grounding for all creative work exploring the power of fun, connection  and our shared human experience as well as developing theatrical awareness. Drawn to what is authentically funny, moving, and true in each individual, Ali enjoys creating welcoming spaces where people can step into the present moment, take creative risks, and embrace a playful way of being.

A qualified teacher (Université de Lille, France, and University of Reading, UK), Ali is a former Head of Performing Arts at St Mary’s Special School and College, Bexhill, and is fluent in English and French.

Alongside co-facilitating Nose to Nose Clowning Levels 1–5, she has delivered training for the Giggle Doctors at Theodora Foundation, Lis’n Tell Speech and Language Practitioners, Quantum Marketing, and medical students at the University of Bristol. She has also worked as a social clown and facilitator at events including The Imaginarium, ARU Cambridge’s Writers’ Conference, and the ANTITHESES Conference at St Hilda’s College, Oxford.
