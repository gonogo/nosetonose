**COURSE DESCRIPTION**What happens when we stop trying to be good and instead become playfully, innocently disobedient?

In clowning rules are everywhere — the rules of games, theatre, politeness, success, and how we think we should appear. The clown listens to them, tries to follow, and then fails, forgets, rebels or invents something more interesting. In breaking rules, the clown reveals what might live beneath them: fear, longing, mischief, tenderness, delight. When these are allowed to appear, the stage becomes more alive — and so do we.

**This five‑day course invites you to explore the clown as a joyful rule‑breaker.**

Through physical play, improvisation and simple performance structures, we’ll discover the freedom that comes from meeting our habits with curiosity and staying connected — to the audience, the group, the moment and to the deeper truth of what is happening.

This is not about being clever, anarchic, or funny on command. It is about discovering the freedom that comes when we meet our habits with curiosity. 

**Over the five days we will explore:**

- **the rules of games**  
  (and the pleasure of misunderstanding them)
- **the rules of objects**(chairs, coats, doors, bags and other everyday conspirators)
- **the rules of social behaviour**(politeness, status, embarrassment and permission)
- **the rules of performance**(entrances, exits, endings, failure and applause)
- **the personal rules that limit our imagination**(“I must be funny”, “I must get it right”, “I must not be too much”, “I must not be seen”)

**This course is for people with some previous clowning or improvisation experience** who wish to deepen their play, become more available on stage, and explore the comic and poetic possibilities of failure, freedom and disobedience.  
**The course is limited to 12 participants.**
