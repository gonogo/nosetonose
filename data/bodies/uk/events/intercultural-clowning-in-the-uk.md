6 – 11 October 2026  
6 day residential – taught in English and French.

[![intercurtural-clown-2013-62-300x225](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/intercurtural-clown-2013-62-300x225.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/intercurtural-clown-2013-62-300x225.jpg)A rare opportunity to work with one of the originators and teachers of our work [Bertil Sylvander](http://www.bataclown.com/spip.php?article43) is a founding member and director of the [Bataclown](http://www.bataclown.com/).

This course is highly recommended to people who have some experience of our work. Participants on this course say that this course is unlike any other clown course they have experienced.

It offers the opportunity for six English speaking participants (previously from Germany, UK and the US) to work with six French participants to explore and celebrate their cultural differences through clowning. The playfulness and authenticity of clowning offers a safe and fun way to meet with what is new and unfamiliar in our lives.

The course limited to 12 people.

**Facilitators:**

**Bertil Sylvander**

Cofondateur du Bataclown, Responsable de formation au Bataclown, acteur-clown et clownanalyste dans la Compagnie “Les Clownanalystes du Bataclown”. Formateur dans les stages du Bataclown (formation de base et formation certifiante “clown et intervention sociale”, coresponsable pédagogique de la filière “Secteur événementiel” ).

![Bertil Sylvander](https://www.bataclown.com/local/cache-vignettes/L350xH230/arton981-a98c1.jpg?1732687055)Il a travaillé le théâtre et l’improvisation, le masque, le mime, la commedia dell’arte, la voix, le clown, le bouffon et le fou du roi avec la Cie “Le Fil à Plomb” (Christian Cailleret), la Cie “Omnibus” (méthode Decroux), Serge Martin, Georges Bonnaud, Hervé Laudières, le Roy Hart Théâtre, etc. Il a travaillé le piano et la clarinette. Gymnastique sensorielle méthode Danis Bois depuis 2006.  
Il a joué dans les spectacles du “Rateau Bavoir” (Fanfare clownesque) de 1978 à 80 et du Bataclown (depuis 1980).  
Metteur en scène de spectacles de clown-théâtre : “*Avec Plaisir*” (Cie les Karamazones, 1988), “*La Papillonne*” (Cie Clown toi-même, 1990), “*La débâcle*” (Cie Nez au vent , 1992), “*Comme un cheveu sur la soupe*” (Cie les Piqués du Nez, 1993), “*Le Grand Poucet*” (Cie Pierre Courtiade, 2008), “*Les grands mythes d’Albert*” (Cie Bulldartis, 2008).  
Cofondateur du Centre de Recherche sur le Clown Contemporain et membre du comité de rédaction de la revue semestrielle Culture clown (2000-2015). Cofondateur de la Fédération Optimiste du Clown d’utilité social (FOCUS-Acteurs-clowns).

### **Vivian Gladwell**

[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21-1024x683.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21-e1760035093978.jpg)Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.  
Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the [Bataclown](http://www.bataclown.com/) in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester ([Clownanalyst](https://www.bataclown.com/-Les-clownanalystes-du-Bataclown-77-.html)).  
He created *Nose to Nose UK* in 1990, *Nose to Nose of North America*in 2006 and *Fool View*, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy –[view history*.*](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (*South Africa, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…*)

He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.
