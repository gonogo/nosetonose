Join Vivian Gladwell at Rudolf Steiner House, London for a one day taster workshop in Nose to Nose Clowning. This will be a light-hearted, playful day exploring creativity, connection and spontaneity whilst discovering your Clown.

Clowning is a state of playfulness that brings us to experience a child-like, naïve and vulnerable state of being. Paradoxically we grow, learn and are strengthened through reconnecting playfully with what makes us most vulnerable. This approach to clowning is not a technique (tricks, gags and routines) but a personal journey towards finding one’s own unique way of being a clown, by listening and being receptive, essential skills for any creative and imaginative expression. It is only when we let go our control over things that we begin to be surprised by the depth of what reveals itself to us. Come for a day of letting go and freeing up!

The group is limited to 14

### Facilitator :

### Vivian Gladwell

[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-2-300x200.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-2.jpg)

Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.  
Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the [**Bataclown**](http://www.bataclown.com/) in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester ([**Clownanalyst**](http://www.clownanalyse.fr/)).  
He created **Nose to Nose UK** in 1990, **Nose to Nose of North America** in 2006 and **Fool View**, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy – **[view history*.*](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)**  
He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (*South Africa**, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…*) He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.
