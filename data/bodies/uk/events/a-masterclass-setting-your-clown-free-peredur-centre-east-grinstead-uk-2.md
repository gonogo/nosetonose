*with Vivian Gladwell[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/netcatching-122x300.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/netcatching.jpg)*

An intensive week for advanced clowns limited to **6 participants**. The course will enable each participant to explore in depth less familiar aspects of clowning with the intention of freeing their clown’s playfulness, creativity and risk taking.

This means one-to-one improvisations with feedback for each participant.

Possible areas of exploration are:

- Rhythm
- The clown’s relation to role and character
- Working with paradox and conflict
- Working with the audience
- Acting versus not acting
- Authenticity
- Pleasure
- The Narrative structure
- Chaos
- The subversive clown
- Surrealism and the absurd
- Tragedy
- Poetry
- … and other discoveries

Prerequisites for the course are Clown level 1 and 2. 

**Facilitator:**

**Vivian Gladwell[![](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21-300x200.jpg)](https://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/Vivian-close-21.jpg)**

Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.

Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the[**Bataclown**](http://www.bataclown.com/) in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester ([**Clownanalyst**](http://www.clownanalyse.fr/)).

He created ***Nose to Nose UK***in 1990, ***Nose to Nose of North America***in 2006 and ***Fool View***, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy – **[view history*.*](http://www.nosetonose.info/briefhistory.htm)**  
He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (*South Africa, USA, Senegal, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…*)

He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.
