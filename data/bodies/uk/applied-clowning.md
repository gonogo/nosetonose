# Play is wiser than words.[flying](http://www.nosetonose.info/uk/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/flying.jpg)

Through play we learn about the world, we create our sense of others and of self, we learn to communicate, we become human. Over the years, Nose to Nose has worked with teachers, social workers, doctors, nurses and therapists. Below are some of the areas we have developed

- [Social Clowning](http://www.nosetonose.info/uk/?p=264 "SOCIAL CLOWNING")
- Teacher training
- Conflict and communication
- Personal development
