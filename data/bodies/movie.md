Nose to Nose at Nowhere conference

Social Clowning at a 'Creative Dialogue' organised
by the Nowhere Academy in London in September 2010. The Creative
Dialogues bring together business leaders and organisational practitioners
in innovative and participatory ways to examine together leading
edge issues for today's organisations and for society. This particular
dialogue focussed on 'Unleashing the Creative Power of Teams'. Sharon
Usher and Vivian Gladwell of Nose to Nose attended the day as participants
and then came on as clowns to improvise with some of the material
of the day. Here is a flavour of the day!
