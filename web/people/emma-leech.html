Background

Emma Leech’s teaching embodies the spirit of Gabrielle Roth, the inspirational founder of 5 Rhythms.

After identifying her potential in London in 1991 , Gabrielle encouraged her, took her to the US to work with her, and funded her training.

For the past 25 years Emma has taught 5 Rhythms in Britain and around the world, always staying true to the original approach she learned direct from Gabrielle.

Recognising the extraordinary power of the practice to heal and to transform lives, she pioneered its use in prisons, refugee camps , addiction recovery clinics in Harley Street to the slums of Kolkata.

Emma is now one of the most skilled and experienced 5 Rhythms instructors in the world.

The 5 Rhythms came into Emma’s world of theatre, visual arts and social justice work over 30 years ago.

Teaching since 2001 her particular flavour is rich with the tapestry woven of “street wise’ and humour, weaving in the edges of the artistic meets’ real life.’
