Background

Andreas has a degree in acting and works as a performer, artistic speaker, presenter and audio artist. Since 2006 he has been training young actors at universities, professional performers and offers numerous workshops and seminars on presence training and storytelling.

He studied at the Berlin University of the Arts and has worked at numerous municipal and state theatres in Germany, including the Maxim Gorki Theatre, Schauspiel Bonn and TuP Essen as well as the Schauspielhaus Bochum and the Opera House Dortmund.

He has been freelancing in DACH since 2006 and has performed with various independent groups and artistic collectives, e.g. kainkollektiv, Drama Köln and subbotnik. Since 2008 he has completed numerous further training courses, e.g. as a somatic movement teacher according to BMC ©, with voice training according to the Lichtenberg method and clown training with Nose to Nose. Currently he’s taking part in their ongoing clowning facilitator training 2024-2026.

Andreas’ work as a performer has received several awards since 1998. He lives in Cologne and speaks German and English fluently.
