UK CLOWN FACILITATOR TRAINING

October 2012 – May 2014

in London, UK

The training essentially reflects our approach and philosophy of teaching clowning. It provides a practical and experiential grounding in the facilitation process.

Length of course

It is a two-year course (October 2012 to May 2014). Each year consists of three 4-day residential modules (Thursday morning to Sunday afternoon) or six modules over two years. A further supervision period of two years is also planned for those who wish to deepen their practice of the work.

Facilitators

Vivian Gladwell and Sharon Usher

Course structure

Each module addresses a different aspect of facilitation and clowning.

· warm-up and its function,
· games
· the structures of impros and how to read them,
· guiding impros, feedback
· group process and the facilitator’s role in the group,
· the clown archetype and its meaning.

Pre-requisites

You will need to have done Clown 1, Clown 2 and Clown 3 as a minimum (preferably the full two-year clown training). Some experience of working with groups and a commitment to your own personal devellopment is necessary.

Applications

Please email facilitator-training@nosetonose.info with:

  • a brief biography
  • your clowning experience
  • your experience of teaching or working with groups
  • your reasons for wanting to do this training
  • how you plan to use this training
