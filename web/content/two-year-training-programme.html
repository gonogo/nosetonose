THE NOSE TO NOSE PROGRAMME A TWO-YEAR CLOWN TRAINING

Nose to Nose offers a coherent two-year programme. The training
takes you through all levels of the work starting with the basics
right up to learning the skills of performance and Social Clowning
(i.e. Court Jester work). You can choose the length of time it takes
to complete this progression but we recommend that you take at least
two-years to allow time for integration. See our programme to choose
your courses.

COURSE OUTLINE

The basic training consists of four separate one-week courses PLUS
five separate Performance weekends.

• Clown 1 (one week or 3 weekends)
• Clown 2 (one week)
• Clown 3 (one week)
• Performance (5 weekends)
• Social Clowning (one week)
