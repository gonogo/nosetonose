Our team

As facilitators, we believe we stay true to the
spirit of clowning when we flow with the creative process of each
person, when we nurture its emotional expression and the emergence
of the imagination and when we facilitate and free actors to find
their own clown, unique and grounded in their relationship to
the audience. It is our belief that by focusing on the individual
we are, in fact, going straight to the heart of clowning which
in the search for authenticity both in the social or personal
dimensions of our lives.
