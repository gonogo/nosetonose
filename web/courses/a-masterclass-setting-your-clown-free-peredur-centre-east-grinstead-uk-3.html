Course description

with Vivian Gladwell

An intensive week for advanced clowns limited to 6 participants. The course will enable each participant to explore in depth less familiar aspects of clowning with the intention of freeing their clown’s playfulness, creativity and risk taking.

This means one-to-one improvisations with feedback for each participant.

Possible areas of exploration are:

  • Rhythm
  • The clown’s relation to role and character
  • Working with paradox and conflict
  • Working with the audience
  • Acting versus not acting
  • Authenticity
  • Pleasure
  • The Narrative structure
  • Chaos
  • The subversive clown
  • Surrealism and the absurd
  • Tragedy
  • Poetry
  • … and other discoveries

Prerequisites for the course are Clown level 1 and 2.
