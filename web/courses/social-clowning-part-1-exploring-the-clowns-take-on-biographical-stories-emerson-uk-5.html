Course description

with Vivian Gladwell and Ali Stockford

The course offers a practical exploration of the clown’s take on biographical stories.

From 2027, this course is now limited to a maximum of 8 participants.

What is Social Clowning?Social Clowning is the use of clowning and play as a way of revealing multiple ways of making sense of the stories we tell. The clown archetype touches on:

  • Authenticity
  • Vulnerability
  • Empowerment
  • Truth
  • Courage
  • Imagination
  • Integrating our shadow
  • Expressing needs
  • Listening
  • Self-expression

The absurd reflection of ourselves and of the world that the clown offers us puts the human heart back at the very centre of our lives.

The group is limited to 8 participants.

PRE-REQUISITE: This course requires participants to have done Clown 1, 2, 3.

Facilitator: Vivian Gladwell and Ali Stockford

Vivian Gladwell

Vivian is the founder and director of Nose to Nose and has given hundreds of workshops around the world. He lives both in the UK and France where he started clowning in 1978. He speaks French and English fluently.
Vivian began teaching in 1988 as part of the Lifeways Festival at Emerson College – UK. He trained as a facilitator with the Bataclown in 1990 and joined their team in 1991 also working as a modern day Court Jester (Clownanalyst).
He created Nose to Nose UK in 1990, Nose to Nose of North America in 2006 and Fool View, the Social Clowning group and has trained facilitators in the UK, the USA, Germany and Italy – view history.
He continues his role as ambassador for the work by researching the applications of clowning in the context of teacher training, medicine, conflict management, ecology and intercultural work in many countries (South Africa, USA, Senegal, China, Taiwan, Vietnam, India, Malaysia, Italy, Germany, Norway…)

He holds clowning as a social playground for exploring our humanity.

Alison (Ali) Stockford

Facilitating  workshops with Nose to Nose since 2016,  Ali holds this work in high esteem as a perfect grounding for all creative work and the bigger picture of trusting ourselves and life.  Always on the lookout for what is authentically funny, touching and true to each individual, Ali enjoys creating spaces for humans to step into the present moment in a playful and connected way.

Performing experience includes: tap dancer and comedy actor in café-théâtre with Les Amuses Gueules Lille, France; Street Theatre and Theatre in Education with Inside Out Theatre Company, Hastings. In addition to nearly 100 workshops for Nose to Nose, Ali has delivered trainings for the Giggle Doctors – Theodora Foundation, Lis’n Tell Speech and Language Practitioners, Quantum Marketing, London, medical students at the University of Bristol and social clowning at The Imaginarium, Cambridge and St Hilda’s College, Oxford  Ali speaks both English and French.
