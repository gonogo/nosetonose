Course description

An Online Course of 6 weekly sessions of 2 hours
*With Vivian Gladwell and Sarah French
January 7, 2027 to February 11, 2027*

In this online course our aim is to use clowning as an inspiration for us as writers. Through games, improvisations and reflection we will give ourselves permission to break rules, to shape-shift, to move into what we do not know, to laugh when we fail and to let go of being “right.” All this to set our imagination free at the service of our writing.

You don’t need to be a writer. You only need to come with open eyes and give your voice to stones and other small things in order to find power in what was silent.

Between sessions, you will be encouraged to write and bring back your stories. 

The group is limited to 12 participants.

WHO IS THIS COURSE  FOR?Anyone with an interest in discovering clowning and/or creative writing and how the two can enhance one another. Beginners and professionals alike are welcome. 

PREREQUISITES:No prerequisites except for wifi access and a computer. Please let us know if you need technical help with Zoom.
