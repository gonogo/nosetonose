Course description:Clowns are shapeshifters, able to transform themselves by playfully interpreting the word around them. They can help us to shift our perspectives and awaken empathy by bringing the world alive.

In this unique course, we will guide you through practical exercises and simple improvisational structures to help you cultivate your skills and deepen your understanding of the art of clowning. 

Identification with objects teaches important clowning principles such as:

  • commitment to physicality and embodiment,
  • use of voice for emotional expression,
  • relationship with partners and audience,
  • personal freedom
  • breaking the rules of theatrical convention
  • exploring roles and breaking free from roles

Come play and let your clown discover the world as new and full of opportunity, absurdity, laughter and poignancy. 

This weekend is open to all levels – whether you’re experienced or new to clowning. 

The group is limited to 12 people.
