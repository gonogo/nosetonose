# Applied clowning

Play is wiser than words.

Through play we learn about the world, we create our sense of others and of self, we learn to communicate, we become human. Over the years, Nose to Nose has worked with teachers, social workers, doctors, nurses and therapists.

# Areas of work

Below are some of the areas we have developed over the years:

- Social Clowning — bringing play and lightness to meetings, conferences, and business events
- Teacher training
- Conflict and communication
- Personal development

# Social Clowning for organisations

Have you ever wished you could lighten up your meetings, business events or conferences in an intelligent, relevant and entertaining way?

Invite Nose to Nose to come as "normal" guests (not as clowns). We mingle, listen, observe and take part. Then, at agreed moments during the day, we reveal ourselves and offer a 5 to 10 minute skit to highlight and summarise the key thoughts and ideas living in the room, much as Court Jesters did in the Middle Ages. There can be up to four such "reviews" during a day event.

We bring cheekiness, impertinence and gentle chaos so as to break through some of the social and institutional conventions and fears that weigh down communication. We get people talking to each other and bring new and creative ways of looking at your issues.

If you want to know more email [Nose to Nose](mailto:mail@nosetonose.info) and we will gladly talk with you.

# Workshops within organisations

Many of our workshops take place within organisations or are custom designed for specific groups. If you feel your organisation could benefit from a theater clowning workshop, contact us and we can put together a program with you.

Our in-house work has included teacher training (intuitive intelligence, pedagogical tact, improvisational readiness), team building, language teacher conferences, circus pedagogy, and workshops on clown and conflict, clown and communication, and clown and trauma.

# Burnout prevention

Increasing work pressure, private and professional double burdens, grey everyday routines, misunderstood communication and lack of recognition are contributing factors to burnout. Total exhaustion can however be avoided by learning to recognise, develop and make optimal use of your own resources.

Through spontaneity and empathy we discover our own playful and creative potential. We strengthen our own perception and appreciation. We learn to be authentic, present and humorous in communication and in our work with clients and colleagues. The challenges of professional life can be met with new lightness, humour and joy. In play we practise trust and acceptance, mobilising inner strengths to find imaginative and divergent solutions in everyday life.

Target groups include anyone exposed to high stress — doctors, teachers, managers, parents, therapists, social workers, and others.

# Integration with heart

An offering for companies and organisations employing refugees and supporting new arrivals in building a new life in Germany.

In a warm interpersonal space, empathy and appreciation can grow — a prerequisite for successful integration of new colleagues seeking protection and building a new existence. Through theatre clowning and improvisation, employees can get to know one another across language and cultural barriers and find a shared, heartfelt language.
